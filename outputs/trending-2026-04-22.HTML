Trending AI Repos

2026-04-22 · Weekly Brief

The skill is the new package. Seven of this week's top fifteen repos ship as drop-in agent skills rather than standalone tools, and the three fastest risers are all design-to-code workflows. awesome-design-md crossing 62K stars in three weeks confirms it: developers want their agents to produce pixel-level UI, not just functional code. Meanwhile, the "open alternative to X" pattern continues unabated—OpenMythos reverse-engineers Claude's architecture, OpenClaude clones its CLI, and open-codesign reproduces its design mode. The meta-game has shifted from building agents to extending them, and from extending them to studying how they work. If you ship an AI product today, assume someone will reconstruct your internals on GitHub by Friday.
