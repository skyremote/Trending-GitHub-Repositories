Editor's Take

Agent memory is this month's infrastructure war. Three of the top entries — MemPalace, Stash, and auto-memory — solve the same problem at different scales: making agents remember across sessions. MemPalace's 49k-star surge in under a month says the market considers this solved at the library level; Stash's Postgres-native approach and auto-memory's CLI-first design show that "where the state lives" is the real design axis. Meanwhile, the 7-day window is dominated by orchestration frameworks (Harmonist, Wanman, Mercury) rather than new model wrappers — builders have stopped chasing the latest checkpoint and started wiring existing models into durable pipelines. The outlier worth watching: text-to-cad, which points AI tooling at mechanical engineering instead of yet another chat interface.
