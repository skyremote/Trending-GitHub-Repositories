Editor's Take

The biggest shift this week: AI is escaping the chat box into physical design and domain workflows. The top new repo turns text prompts into parametric CAD models, not another chatbot skin. Meanwhile, the agent-orchestration space is splitting into two camps — zero-dependency portable runtimes (Harmonist, Wanman) versus persistent-memory stacks that lean on Postgres (Stash). Both bets are credible; neither has won. On the 30-day chart, skill-economy repos continue to dominate: career coaches, knowledge-graph builders, and creative-writing pipelines all packaged as distributable Claude Code skills. The community has clearly decided that the atomic unit of AI tooling is the skill, not the model. Star velocity on Caveman (31k to 48k in two weeks) proves that developer ergonomics — even absurd ones — still beat raw capability announcements.
