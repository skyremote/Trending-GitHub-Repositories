Editor's Take

The AI tooling market is forking into two clear camps: those building agents that do things (legal, trading, security, ops) and those building the scaffolding to run agents cheaper or evaluate them better. Meanwhile, vertical agents like mike (law) and RunbookHermes (incident response) are proving that domain-specific agents outperform generalists when the workflow is well-scoped. The 30-day window tells the same story at scale: OpenMythos and obscura exist because people want to rebuild the infrastructure, not rent it.
