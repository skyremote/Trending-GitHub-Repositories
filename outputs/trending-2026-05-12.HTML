Editor's Take

The AI-agent ecosystem is splitting into two clear layers this week. The infrastructure layer is hardening: virtual filesystems for sandboxed agents, headless browsers purpose-built for LLM navigation, and inference engines chasing theoretical throughput ceilings. Meanwhile, the application layer is going vertical fast — AI-powered CAD, legal platforms, trading orchestration, and even cell-biology visualization. The GPT-Image-2 prompt-library gold rush continues from last month, with three separate "awesome" collections competing for the same audience, which says more about demand than originality. Anthropic quietly shipped a long-running agents repo, a signal that persistent multi-session agents are moving from experiment to supported pattern. The most consequential trend: agent evaluation tooling is catching up to agent creation tooling. Teams that instrument early will compound their advantage.
