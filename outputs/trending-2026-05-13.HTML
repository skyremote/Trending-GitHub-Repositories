Editor's Take

Two distinct currents this week. In the 7-day window, 3DCellForge pulled 1,725 stars in three days by applying generative AI to scientific 3D cell visualization — a reminder that the highest-growth pockets are often outside the usual chatbot and coding-assistant categories. The agent ecosystem is consolidating around Hermes: three of the top ten new repos build tooling specifically for that framework. DeepSeek V4 Pro's desktop client appeared within a day of the model's announcement, confirming that open-weight model communities now ship consumer wrappers at near-real-time speed.

In the 30-day window, GPT-Image-2 prompt libraries dominate — three separate repos collectively hold 25,000+ stars. But the real story is OpenMythos at 12,500 stars: a from-scratch theoretical reconstruction of Claude's architecture that has attracted serious research attention. Browser Harness from the browser-use team at 12,340 stars confirms browser automation for agents has graduated from demo to infrastructure.

The thread connecting both windows: the community is spending less time building new models and more time building surfaces, workflows, and evaluation rigs for existing ones. We are firmly in the application layer.
