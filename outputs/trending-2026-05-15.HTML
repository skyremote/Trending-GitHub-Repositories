Editor's Take

The AI open-source landscape this week is dominated by two themes: agents that do real work in narrow domains, and infrastructure that makes agents cheaper to run. The standout is kyegomez/OpenMythos, which shot to nearly 13,000 stars in under a month by reverse-engineering Claude's Mythos architecture from published research. Whether or not its reconstruction is accurate, the appetite for understanding frontier model internals is clearly massive. Meanwhile, the 7-day chart shows a shift from "yet another chatbot wrapper" toward tools with tangible output: 3D model studios, CAD pipelines, and multi-agent investment analysts that debate each other before placing trades. The agent-skills ecosystem (Agentara, OpenClaw) is maturing fast, and LLM infrastructure layers like llmix and outputguard signal that developers are past the prototype phase and solving production pain. The most interesting undercurrent: Chinese-language AI tooling is surging, with multiple A-share data tools and prompt libraries cracking the top charts.
