# Trending AI Repositories — 2026-05-16

## Editor's Take

The AI agent ecosystem is consolidating around a clear pattern: specialized skills plugged into general-purpose hosts. This week's standouts — `html-anything`, `agentara/skills`, `journal-adapt-writing-skill` — all ship as composable modules rather than monolithic apps. Meanwhile, `OpenMythos` crossing 12k stars in under a month signals that the community wants to reverse-engineer frontier model architectures in the open, not just consume their APIs. The most under-appreciated trend is AI tooling arriving in domains that never asked for it: graph overlays for Postgres (`pgGraph`), CAD pipelines (`text-to-cad`), and IoT firmware agents (`esp-claw`). Infrastructure is eating the agent stack.
