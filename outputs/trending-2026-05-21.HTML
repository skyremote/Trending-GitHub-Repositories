Editor's Take
The agent ecosystem is stratifying fast. This week's standout, Vercel's zerolang, signals that agent orchestration is moving from prompt chains into dedicated programming languages — a bet that the current Python-script-plus-tool-calling pattern won't survive at scale. Meanwhile, the 30-day window tells a quieter story: the tools gaining the most traction aren't agents themselves but the surfaces agents produce on (slide decks, legal documents, 3D models) and the infrastructure they depend on (virtual filesystems, orchestration runtimes). If you're building agent tooling, the market is telling you that developers want fewer abstractions around the LLM call and more around the output artifact. The "agent skill" as a distributable unit — pioneered by Claude Code's ecosystem — now accounts for four of the fifteen repos across both lists.
