Editor's Take

The agent-as-platform thesis won this month. Four of the top five 30-day repos are not models or training scripts — they are surfaces, filesystems, and runtimes that treat LLMs as an assumed primitive. Meanwhile, the 7-day window shows a sharp turn toward evaluation and benchmarking: ASR rescoring, spatial VQA, video-frame sampling, and multimodal probing toolkits all broke through in the same week. The implication is clear — builders have moved past "can we make it work" into "how do we know it works." The most interesting signal is ADHD, a tree-of-thought skill with pruning for coding agents. Structured divergent reasoning inside an agent loop is a pattern worth watching; it treats the agent itself as a search problem, not a pipeline.
