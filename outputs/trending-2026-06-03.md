# Trending AI Repos — 2026-06-03

## Editor's take

The dominant signal this week is that AI agents are no longer a feature — they are the deployment unit. Eight of fifteen repos ship agent-native infrastructure: memory layers, filesystem sandboxes, control planes, competitive pairing harnesses. The tooling layer has visibly hardened from "prompt wrapper" to "operating system." Two repos stand out for structural reasons. `memory-os` and `vigils` represent the two sides of the same coin: one gives agents durable recall across sessions, the other gives humans a kill switch and audit trail. The market is pricing in that agents will run unsupervised, and it is simultaneously building the trust infrastructure to make that tolerable. Meanwhile, Alibaba shipping `modelstudioai/cli` as a structured-tool-call-native CLI — not a chat wrapper — signals that even large vendors have accepted the agent-framework interface as the default consumption surface for foundation models. If you are still building model wrappers with REST endpoints and prompt templates, you are building for 2024.
