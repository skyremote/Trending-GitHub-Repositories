Editor's Take

The AI open-source scene this week is defined by two forces pulling in opposite directions: consolidation at the infrastructure layer and fragmentation at the tooling layer. Alibaba's open-code-review continues to vacuum up stars by proving that enterprise-grade AI code review can be open-sourced without losing its teeth. Meanwhile, the 7-day chart is dominated by MCP servers, agent orchestrators, and adapter shims — a clear sign that practitioners have moved past "which model" and are now fighting over "which plumbing." The most technically interesting new entry is tessera, a from-scratch LLM distillation and serving engine with custom Triton/CUDA kernels and a Rust gateway. If you only look at one repo this week, make it smallcode — an AI coding agent that hits 87% on benchmarks with a 4B-parameter model, which says more about the state of efficient inference than any whitepaper.
