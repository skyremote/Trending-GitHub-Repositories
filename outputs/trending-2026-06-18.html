Editor's Take

The agent-skills ecosystem has hit escape velocity. Eight of fifteen repos this week are packaged skills or harnesses, not standalone tools. The defining project is ponytail, which racked up 32k stars in six days by codifying a contrarian philosophy: the best agent work is the work the agent avoids doing. Meanwhile, Vercel entering the agent-framework space with eve signals that building agents is about to look more like building web apps. On the 30-day radar, odysseus at 73k stars is the clearest sign yet that developers want self-hosted AI workspaces, not SaaS subscriptions. The cross-vendor agent loop (architect-loop, omnigent) is emerging as a genuine pattern: route planning to one model, execution to another, verification to a third.
