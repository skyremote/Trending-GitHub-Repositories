Editor's Take

The signal this week is unmistakable: the AI ecosystem has moved past "build an agent" into "build the world around agents." Five of the top ten 7-day repos are infrastructure plays — memory, orchestration, search, observability — rather than agents themselves. Odysseus obliterating 76K stars in under a month as a self-hosted workspace confirms that developers want to own their AI stack, not rent it. Meanwhile, ponytail's 52K stars for a skill that tells your agent to write less code is the most honest market signal in months — the bottleneck is no longer generation but restraint. The new entrants worth watching: agent-apprenticeship is betting that agents can learn from each other's traces, and PixelRAG is killing the text-parsing pipeline entirely by searching screenshots instead of DOM trees. If you are still building traditional RAG with chunked text, both of these repos are your leading indicators.
