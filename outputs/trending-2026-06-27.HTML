Editor's Take

The agent-skills marketplace has arrived. Six of the week's top ten repos are installable skill packs — video production, motion graphics, browser search, bio-science — not frameworks, not wrappers, just drop-in capabilities you point an agent at. The supply side of the agent economy is now moving faster than the demand side. Meanwhile, the 30-day list tells a starker story: Odysseus and Ponytail each pulled more stars in a month than most developer tools collect in a year, signaling that self-hosted AI workspaces and opinionated agent philosophies have crossed from niche tooling into mass-market adoption. The quiet winner this week is Qwen-AgentWorld, which trains agents inside simulated environments rather than on static datasets — a methodological shift that, if it scales, makes every benchmark built on log replay look dated. If you are still shipping agent frameworks without an eval story, the window is closing; the community now expects measurement alongside capability.
