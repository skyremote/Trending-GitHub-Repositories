Editor's Take

The AI open-source landscape this week is dominated by a clear thesis: agents need better infrastructure, not more wrappers. The standout 7-day entry, theeleven, shows autonomous AI agents breaking into real-money DeFi markets — a concrete use case while most agent projects are still demo-ware. Meanwhile, Godcoder suggests developers are tired of cloud-locked coding assistants and want local-first alternatives they actually control.

On the 30-day horizon, the sheer velocity of odysseus (78K+ stars) signals massive pent-up demand for self-hosted AI workspaces. ponytail took a different angle — not building new AI capabilities but constraining them, teaching agents to write less code rather than more. That it hit 60K+ stars says something about where the pain actually is.

The most technically interesting entry is Xiaomi's MiMo-Code, which treats model training and agent behavior as a co-evolution problem rather than separate concerns. Three themes to watch: agent evaluation, agent memory, and the browser as an agent runtime.
