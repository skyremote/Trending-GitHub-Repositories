## Editor's take

The AI tooling landscape has split into two distinct layers this week. On top, agent *orchestration* — meta-harnesses, skills registries, and @-mention routers that treat individual coding agents as interchangeable backends. Below, agent *infrastructure* — memory systems, behavior cloning, and evaluation frameworks that make those agents reliable enough to orchestrate in the first place. The week's standout signal: Odysseus hit 79k stars in under a month as a self-hosted AI workspace, confirming that developers want to own the full stack rather than rent it. Meanwhile, the "skills" pattern (reusable, installable agent capabilities) has moved from experiment to standard — four of the top weekly repos are skills packages for different verticals. If you are building an AI tool and it does not expose a skills interface, you are building a dead end.
