✎ Editor's Take

The AI-agent tooling layer is consolidating fast. This week's standout signal: developers are no longer debating whether to use coding agents — they are building the connective tissue around them. Merge queues for parallel agents, token-efficiency skills, judgment layers, and meta-harnesses that swap one agent for another without rewriting.

The 30-day chart tells the same story at scale: ponytail (76k stars in three weeks) is a set of system prompts that tells your agent to write less code, and it is the most-starred new repo of the month. The entire top five is infrastructure for agents, not agents themselves.

Two other threads worth watching. First, AI-for-science is splitting into competing camps (openscience vs. open-science) days after Anthropic shipped Claude Science — both positioning as the open, model-agnostic alternative. Second, hardware-level AI continues to surface: gateGPT implements a full transformer in Verilog on an FPGA, running inference at 56k tokens/second with zero software stack.
