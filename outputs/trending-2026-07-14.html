Meanwhile, the agent ecosystem continues splitting into two camps: infrastructure (auth gateways, GPU networks, Kubernetes operators) and introspection (profile miners, self-learning skills, loop-until-verified frameworks). If you are building a new agent framework, the moat is not the loop; it is the skill marketplace.
