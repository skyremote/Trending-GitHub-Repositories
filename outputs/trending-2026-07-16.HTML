Editor's Take

The AI open-source scene this week is dominated by agents that do real jobs, not demos. T3MP3ST (4.8k stars in two weeks) is a full autonomous red-teaming harness — the kind of tool security teams actually need now that every org runs LLM-powered services. Open-Connector (2.6k stars) solves the boring-but-critical auth layer between AI agents and the SaaS tools they need to touch. Meanwhile, Flawless (644 stars in under a week) brings the same agentic pattern to Kubernetes SRE.

The other strong signal: AI introspection tooling. Kill-AI-Slop is an agent skill that scans your own product for telltale AI-generated tics. J-Wash gives researchers direct Jacobian-based control over LLM alignment. And inference-school teaches Metal-native LLM inference from first principles in Swift.

The agent ecosystem is maturing fast. The projects gaining traction are infrastructure, security tools, and engineering workflows built for production.
