# Trending AI Repositories — 2026-07-16

## Editor's Take

The AI open-source scene this week is dominated by **agents that do real jobs**, not demos. T3MP3ST (4.8k stars in two weeks) is a full autonomous red-teaming harness — the kind of tool security teams actually need now that every org runs LLM-powered services. Open-Connector (2.6k stars) solves the boring-but-critical auth layer between AI agents and the SaaS tools they need to touch. Meanwhile, Flawless (644 stars in under a week) brings the same agentic pattern to Kubernetes SRE, and AWS shipped SpecShip as a reference architecture for spec-driven autonomous coding workflows.

The other strong signal: **AI introspection tooling**. Kill-AI-Slop is an agent skill that scans your own product for the telltale visual and copy tics of AI-generated content — essentially AI auditing itself. J-Wash gives researchers direct Jacobian-based control over LLM alignment. And inference-school teaches Metal-native LLM inference from first principles in Swift, targeting Apple silicon developers who want to understand the stack they ship on.

The agent ecosystem is maturing fast. The projects gaining traction are no longer "chat with your docs" wrappers. They are infrastructure, security tools, and engineering workflows built for production.
