Editor's Take

The agent ecosystem is splitting into two distinct layers: infrastructure and taste. Infrastructure projects like Open Connector (auth gateway for 1,000+ SaaS endpoints) and sim-use (mobile-simulator eyes and hands) are solving the plumbing that lets agents touch the real world. Meanwhile, a new class of "taste" tools — kill-ai-slop, self-learning-skills, and Brain0's provenance tracking — exist solely to make AI-written output less terrible and more auditable.

The most interesting signal this week is J-Wash, which hands users Anthropic's own Jacobian Lens to manually edit model alignment. That a tool for un-aligning models can gain 170+ stars in four days says something about where the community's curiosity is pointing. Science workbenches (openscience, open-science) are suddenly a two-horse race, both born the same week. If you ship AI tools, the takeaway is clear: the market now expects your output to be traceable, your integrations to be turnkey, and your defaults to be tasteful.
