Editor's Take

The coding agent wars have a new front: infrastructure. Grok's open-source harness hit 23k stars in under three weeks, and AOS shipped an entire agent operating system at 8k — both in Rust, both positioning themselves as the substrate everyone else builds on top of. Meanwhile, on the application layer, the week's signal is that single-prompt generation is maturing from party trick to proof of capability: a full FPS game, a space RTS, and a book collection page all shipped as one-shot Claude outputs, racking up thousands of stars apiece. The practical tools gaining ground are smaller and more opinionated — a 426-token agent memory prompt, a YAGNI-enforcing skill, a dependency-free C engine that streams 2.78T model weights from NVMe. The pattern: the infrastructure is getting bigger while the interventions are getting smaller.
