Editor's Take

The agent-skill ecosystem has eclipsed the agent itself as the unit of competition. Four of this week's top repos are skills or plugins — slide generators, writing-style enforcers, video shot recipes — designed to plug into whichever harness the developer already chose. The harness wars may be settling; the skill wars are just starting.

Meanwhile, the "run a trillion-parameter model on commodity hardware" trend keeps compressing. kimi-k3-in-c fits a 2.78T-parameter model into 8 GB of RAM with zero dependencies, and turbo-fieldfare runs Gemma 4 26B in 2 GB on a MacBook. The message to cloud providers: inference margins face a structural squeeze from below.

The other notable signal is office suites. GenOffice, HermesOffice, and the Kimi PPT skill all shipped in the same month. Document creation — not chat, not code — may be the next surface where agents feel inevitable to ordinary users.
