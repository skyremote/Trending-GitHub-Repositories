Editor's Take

The "skill" has landed as a distribution format. Five of this week's top ten repos are packaged skills for coding agents — writing style, growth hacking, offensive security, video production, novel characters — and the pattern spans Claude Code, Codex, and Copilot alike. This is no longer an experiment; it is the new pip install for agent capabilities. The 30-day picture is dominated by full-stack agent harnesses (grok-build, qm, AOS), not single-purpose wrappers, meaning the industry is consolidating around thick runtimes that own the loop. The "agent as office worker" metaphor is gaining real traction: two AI-native office suites and an agentic CRM all crossed 2k stars this month. On the inference side, Kimi K3's 2.78-trillion parameters now run on a single CPU, and Gemma 4 fits in 2 GB on a MacBook. Hardware requirements are becoming a rounding error.
