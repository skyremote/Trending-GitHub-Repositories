Editor’s Take

The skills marketplace has cracked open. Half of this week’s top repos are packaged skills for Claude Code or Codex, not standalone tools — writing style, slide generation, video production, character design. The shift is structural: developers are building on agent harnesses instead of against raw APIs. Meanwhile, the 30-day view shows full applications being rebuilt agent-first: a CRM, an office suite, a voice runtime. The interesting tension is between “skill as sharable atom” (human-writing, shuohao-skills) and “monolith with AI baked in” (GenOffice, Comp AI CRM). Both are gaining stars; neither has won. If you ship developer tools, the skill distribution problem — discovery, trust, versioning — is the underserved gap right now.
