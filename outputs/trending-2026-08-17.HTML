Editor's Take

The AI open-source landscape this week has a split personality. On one side, infrastructure is maturing fast: Vercel shipped a software-factory template, shadcn-ui released a production-ready chatbot scaffold, and a new reference architecture for safe agent pipelines landed with nearly 500 stars in four days. The DeepSeek Harness ecosystem exploded with curated lists, desktop apps, and handbooks — a clear sign that DSH is crossing from early adopter to mainstream tooling.

On the other side, a repo for stripping AI provenance watermarks rocketed past 11,000 stars in under a week, making it the fastest-growing project in this report by a wide margin. That says something uncomfortable about demand. Meanwhile, the 30-day view shows the bigger structural shifts: YC-backed qm is building a multiplayer agent harness, Comp AI open-sourced an agent-first CRM, and GenSpark launched a full AI office suite. The theme across both windows is the same — the industry is no longer asking "should we use AI agents?" but "how do we operate, secure, and scale them?"
