Editor's Take

The harness pattern has won the week. Three of the top ten 7-day repos wrap an LLM around a device — your Mac, your webcam, your phone — and let the model drive it directly. Meanwhile, DeepSeek Harness (DSH) is experiencing its own Cambrian explosion: a handbook, two competing desktop apps, a plugin marketplace, and a cost meter all appeared in seven days, mirroring what happened around Claude Code three months ago. The most telling 30-day signal is watermarks-remover at 14,600 stars — the developer community is actively pushing back against AI provenance mandates before the ink is dry. On the constructive side, agent-safe-pipeline shows that safety-conscious architecture is getting built alongside the evasion tooling, not after it. Hardware finally gets a seat at the table with an FPGA inference chip running a real transformer decoder, bit-exact against a golden model. The month belongs to whoever is building the connective tissue between agents — routing, safety, memory, team coordination — not to whoever ships the next model wrapper.
