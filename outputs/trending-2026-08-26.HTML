Editor's Take

Agents stopped being tools this week and started being colleagues. Cumora and OpenBot both crossed 2,900 stars in under ten days by treating AI agents as first-class team members — one seats them in chat, the other gives each one its own computer. Meanwhile, the harness war is now genuinely three-way: DeepSeek Harness shipped a full ecosystem in two weeks, while browser-use extended to macOS-wide control and HarnessRouter published a Unified Harness Protocol to bridge them all. The routing layer above harnesses is the new battleground — sprix-sage-router’s SELF/COLLABORATE/HANDOFF state machine is the first serious attempt at standardizing how agents decide when to call each other. If you are building single-agent tooling, you are already behind the multi-agent coordination curve.
