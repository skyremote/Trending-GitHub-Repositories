Watermark removal is the month's runaway category — two repos, one clearing 18k stars — which tells you more about market demand for unmarked AI content than about any technical breakthrough. The real signal this week is agent infrastructure hardening: governance layers (`halofy`), email primitives (`sentio`), and debugger integration (`x64dbg-mcp-server`) are the kind of plumbing that only gets built once agents are running in production, not demos. Nobody has won that race, and the longer it runs without a standard, the more likely MCP eats the coordination layer instead.
