The coding-agent ecosystem has entered its middleware era. Six of the top ten weekly repos are not agents themselves but tools that sit between agents and the work — context relays, recovery controllers, Datalog memory layers, cross-harness bridges. The raw “build another agent” wave has crested; now the market is figuring out how to make existing agents reliable.
