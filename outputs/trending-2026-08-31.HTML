Editor's Take

The coding agent is no longer the product — the skill you bolt onto it is. Six of the ten 7-day repos are skills, gateways, or harness wrappers, not new agents. The most starred newcomer, codex-with-chatgpt, does not build a new agent at all; it plugs ChatGPT into the existing Codex harness as a planning layer. Meanwhile sepia (de-AI your writing), fire-your-seo-agency (automate SEO audits), and opengrok (swap models inside Grok) treat the agent as a socket and compete on what you plug in. The 30-day view confirms the pattern at scale: CopilotKit's OpenBot and cumora both bet that agents work best as teammates inside a larger system, not standalone binaries. Infrastructure play of the week is lemmalog — a Datalog engine as shared memory for agents — signaling the ecosystem moving past key-value stores into structured reasoning over stored facts.
