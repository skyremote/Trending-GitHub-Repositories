Editor's take: The agent ecosystem has crossed into its org-chart phase. This week's signal: people are no longer building individual agents — they are building agent companies (headcount), agent team chats (cumora), and agent coworkers with their own computers (OpenBot, useagent). The underlying infrastructure has shifted from "how do I call an LLM" to "how do I manage a workforce that happens to be software." Meanwhile, a parallel counter-movement is gaining speed: de-AI writing tools (sepia, human-writing) and watermark strippers are now among the highest-starred new repos, suggesting users want AI's output without AI's fingerprint. The practical takeaway is that the skill marketplace — not the model provider — is becoming the unit of competition.
