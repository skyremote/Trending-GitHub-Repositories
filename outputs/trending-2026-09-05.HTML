Editor's Take

The agent ecosystem has fully bifurcated. One camp builds agents that control physical devices — phones, Macs, browsers — while the other builds agents that control other agents. Both are hiring for the same infra layer: routing, memory, and continual learning. The clearest signal this week is that anti-hallucination and anti-laziness tooling now attracts more stars than the agents themselves. When the guardrails outpace the capabilities, the market is telling you trust is the bottleneck, not intelligence.
