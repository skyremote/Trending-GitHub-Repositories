# Trending AI Repositories — 2026-09-06

## Editor's Take

The agent ecosystem is consolidating fast. This week's standout is Anthropic open-sourcing a full commerce-agent blueprint — not a toy demo, but a production reference with retail, telecom, and entertainment verticals already wired up. That repo hit 2,000 stars in five days. Meanwhile, the broader 30-day picture tells a different story: developers are less interested in building new agents than in fixing the ones they have. Three of the top five monthly repos (`unlazy`, `sepia`, `reverify`) exist solely to make agents stop cutting corners, stop sounding like robots, and stop hallucinating. The meta-tooling layer is maturing. If you are building on agents today, the question is no longer "which framework?" but "which guardrails?"
