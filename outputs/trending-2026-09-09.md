# Trending AI Repositories — 2026-09-09

## Editor's Take

The AI ecosystem this week is less about model improvements and more about the infrastructure settling around agents. Memory — how agents persist knowledge across sessions — is the clear obsession, with multiple projects (OKF Agent Memory, VoiceMem, agent-memory, EverRoom) all racing to solve the same problem from different angles. Meanwhile, the watermarks-remover repo quietly became the month's breakout at 21k+ stars, exposing a growing tension between AI provenance standards and user ownership. The other major thread: developers are building real, playful things with AI — modding SNES games live, generating Pokemon battle cinematics, standing up personal video streaming. The tools are maturing past productivity wrappers into creative territory. Worth watching: the OKF (Open Knowledge Format) standard picking up steam for agent-native knowledge, and Vercel's eve template signaling that software factories are about to get commoditized.
