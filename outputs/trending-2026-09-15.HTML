Editor's Take

The developer tooling layer around AI agents is thickening fast. This week's standout pattern: repos that treat the AI coding assistant itself as a platform surface — extracting its session data, routing its model calls, mapping architecture before it edits, and masking PII in transit. The agent-as-OS thesis is also gaining conviction with two independent takes (Rune and AgentVerse-OS) landing in the same seven-day window.

The 30-day view confirms that multi-agent orchestration has moved from demos to opinionated production infrastructure: OpenBot gives each agent its own computer, Sprix Sage Router solves the handoff problem nobody wanted to solve manually, and Cumora treats agents as chat participants rather than API endpoints. Embodied AI is the sleeper category — two repos shipping real teleoperation data pipelines suggest the training-data bottleneck for robotics is starting to crack.
