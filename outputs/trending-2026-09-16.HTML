Editor's Take

The week's clearest signal: AI agent workspaces are graduating from plugins to operating systems. AgentVerse-OS ships a full windowed desktop for humans and agents on one server; Rune rebuilds the terminal multiplexer around agent orchestration. Developers are simultaneously demanding transparency into what those agents did — ai-data-extractor pulls chat histories out of every major coding assistant, and Birdview maps architecture before an agent touches a file.

The 30-day view confirms MCP has become assumed infrastructure (codex-with-chatgpt, chat-on-steroids, and x64dbg-mcp-server all treat it as plumbing, not novelty), while Sprix Sage Router's 3,690 stars prove that multi-agent routing is now an engineering problem with real adopters, not a research curiosity. One counter-trend worth watching: Sepia, a tool for removing AI artifacts from writing, hit 2,631 stars — the market for making AI output less obviously AI is growing as fast as the market for producing it.
