Editor's Take

The agent ecosystem is stratifying. This week's top repos split into two clear tiers: infrastructure that agents run on (memory, OS, audit trails) and tools that make agents useful to non-developers (browser agents, architecture maps, Siri delegation). The most telling signal is ai-data-extractor leading the 7-day chart — people want to understand what their coding agents actually did, not just what they shipped. The tooling layer is maturing faster than the models underneath it.
