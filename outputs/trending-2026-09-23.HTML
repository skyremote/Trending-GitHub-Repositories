Editor's Take

This week belongs to TypeSafe AI's Jev. The "System One" typed decision model — a small, fast network that returns calibrated probability distributions instead of text tokens — appeared six days ago and already dominates seven of the top ten weekly slots. The pattern is familiar: a new primitive drops, an ecosystem forms overnight, and awesome-lists race to catalogue it before the dust settles. What makes Jev worth watching beyond the hype cycle is the category it opens: sub-50ms decision calls that let agents pick models, gauge intent, and route requests without burning output tokens. laya-mlx running at 7ms on an M3 Max and jev-chat-jarvis hitting 4k stars in two days suggest the use case is real, not just curiosity traffic. Meanwhile, the 30-day chart tells a quieter story: self-improving agents (reef, NeoHorse) and agent-skill libraries (sepia, screenwriting-skills) are maturing into production infrastructure. The market is splitting into models that think and models that decide — and this week, deciding pulled ahead.
