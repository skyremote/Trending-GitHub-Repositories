The "dots" wave arrived. Three of the top ten weekly repos are open-source clones or riffs on autonomous AI agents with their own browsers and desktops — OpenDots, dots, and open-dot all shipped within 48 hours of each other. That tells you the market considers persistent, browser-equipped agents a solved-enough pattern to commoditize.
