Editor's Take

MCP is eating creative tooling. This week's standout is a Blender addon that turns a 3D editor into an AI-driven scene-building pipeline — and it pulled nearly 800 stars in four days. The broader pattern: AI agents are colonizing domain-specific desktop software (Blender, game engines, even iPhone control via a $7 ESP32) rather than staying inside chat windows and IDEs. Meanwhile, the “agent skill” format pioneered by Claude Code is becoming a distribution channel in its own right — three of the month's top repos are installable skills, not standalone apps. If you are still building tools that require users to leave their workflow, you are building for last quarter.
