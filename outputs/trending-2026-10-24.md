## Editor's Take

The AI coding-agent ecosystem is consolidating fast. This week's standout signal: the tools *around* agents now attract more stars than the agents themselves. Meanwhile, AIHOT (8.5k stars) automates the entire content-curation pipeline with LLMs, and OpenDots blurs the line between text chat, voice calls, and Slack into one always-on AI coworker layer.

On the infrastructure side, the "run big models on small hardware" trend keeps accelerating. StartLux Decision ships calibrated probability outputs from models as small as 0.8B parameters — useful for agent routing where you need a fast, confident yes/no before calling a heavier model.

The week's sleeper hit is MemT-1.0 (374 stars, 7 days old): a native long-term memory layer for LLMs that plugs into DeepSeek and Qwen via SGLang. If it works as advertised, it solves one of the persistent pain points in multi-session agent workflows without bolting on an external vector store.
