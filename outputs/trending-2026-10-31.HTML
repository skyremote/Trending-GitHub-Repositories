The agent ecosystem has split into two clear tiers this week. Tier one is physical: three of the top repos bridge AI to real hardware — an Android phone agent, an ESP32-to-iPhone rig, and a digital-human video pipeline that turns a single photo into a talking head. Tier two is defensive: BLA ships adversarial shielding for agent binaries, grim-prompter generates threat models for coding agents, and agent-purge lets you swap system prompts across seven competing agent CLIs from one config. The implication is that people building agents have moved past “can it work” to “can it survive in the wild.” Meanwhile, the 30-day window shows answer-me-with-html pulling nearly five thousand stars for turning LLM answers into one-page interactive HTML — a pattern that may quietly obsolete the chatbot transcript as a default output format.
