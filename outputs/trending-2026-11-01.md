# Trending AI Repositories — 2026-11-01

## Editor's Take

The last week belongs to on-device AI agents. Three of the top five repos put a full agent runtime on a phone — Android and iOS alike — without cloud calls, root access, or developer accounts. DeepSeek alone shipped two mobile harnesses and an autonomous "superagent" prototype in seven days, signaling that the Chinese lab sees edge-first agents as the next battlefield, not just inference endpoints.

Meanwhile, the agent-skills ecosystem continues to mature. Theo Browne's `tstack` and Heydamir's `unslop` show that the developer community now treats agent behavior as composable middleware: one skill polishes AI-generated UI into production design, another bundles an opinionated daily workflow. Security tooling is keeping pace — `grim-prompter` and `sapper-v2` both frame AI agents as attack surfaces worth formal threat modeling.

On the monthly chart, `gpu-unlock` is the breakout story: a driver-level patch that doubles tensor throughput on consumer GeForce cards. It democratizes local AI in a way that no amount of API price cuts can match. Microsoft's `TokenEconomizer` tackles the other side of the cost equation, bringing experience-driven cost control to coding agents that would otherwise burn through token budgets unchecked.

The signal is clear: AI is leaving the cloud. The repos that gained the most stars this month are the ones that put models, agents, and controls directly on the hardware people already own.
