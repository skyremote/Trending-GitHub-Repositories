# Trending AI Repos — 2026-11-12

## Editor's take

The phone is the new frontier for AI agents. Three of this week's top ten repos — Pinky, Poki, and Vork — are dedicated to giving agents control of physical devices and real-world rendering pipelines. This isn't the "chatbot on your phone" wave from two years ago; these projects hand agents the screen, the cameras, and the GPU, then ask a human to approve before anything ships. The same pattern shows up in the 30-day window: dsh-Go wraps DeepSeek's harness in a Flutter app, and Mika operates Android apps through accessibility APIs. Agents are moving from the terminal to the touchscreen, and the biggest unsolved problem is no longer capability — it's trust boundaries on hardware you carry in your pocket.

Meanwhile, the tooling layer around coding agents is maturing fast. Agent-autoloop, maxagent, and tinker-ai-skill each solve a distinct gap — background autonomy, persistent memory, and self-improvement — that early adopters have been duct-taping with shell scripts. The fact that these gaps now have purpose-built repos with thousands of stars says the "agent harness" is becoming its own software category.
