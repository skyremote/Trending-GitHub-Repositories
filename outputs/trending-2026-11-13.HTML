Editor's Take

The agent-skill gold rush is real and accelerating. Six of this week's top ten new repos are packaged skills or hooks for coding agents — not libraries, not frameworks, but drop-in behaviour modules that treat Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode as platforms to extend. The signal: the "app store" layer for AI agents is forming in the open, repo by repo, before any vendor ships one. Meanwhile, DeepSeek quietly released a superagent prototype and a mobile harness that together drew 43k stars in under two weeks, the clearest sign yet that the agent-infrastructure race is no longer Claude-vs-GPT but a three-way competition.
