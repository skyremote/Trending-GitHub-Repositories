Daily brief — GitHub's fastest-moving AI projects this week and month

Editor's Take

Agent skills are eating the world. Six of the top ten 7-day repos are skills — packaged instructions that teach a coding agent to do one thing well. We have moved from "build me an agent" to "build me a skill for the agent I already have." The practical consequence: agent harnesses (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) are becoming operating systems, and skills are their app stores. Meanwhile the 30-day chart is dominated by DeepSeek's infrastructure plays — DeepInfer and dsh-superagent-prototype together hold nearly 50K stars — confirming that the inference-cost war and the agent-autonomy race are running in tandem. Zagitta's gpu-unlock hitting 19K stars in under a month shows how much latent demand exists for cheaper local AI compute. The meta story: the stack is bifurcating into infrastructure people pay for at the bottom and skills people share for free at the top.
