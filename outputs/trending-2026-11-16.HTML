Editor's Take

The "agent as desktop companion" pattern is consolidating. Three of this week's top repos — yogurt (meeting notes), agentes-chat (team chat), and linger (personal agent) — all bet on local-first AI that lives alongside your existing workflows rather than replacing them. Meanwhile, GitHub's trending charts were polluted by two spam campaigns: a Blender MCP clone army (~15 identical repos from fresh accounts, all created Nov 12 within minutes of each other) and a Gemini config-checker fork ring (~30 copies). If you use star counts as a discovery signal, budget time for deduplication — the astroturfing infrastructure is getting more sophisticated.

The genuine gems this week are infrastructure plays: Huawei's MACaQ pushing GGUF quantization to new SOTA across modalities, and gpucanvas building the GPU-rendered infinite canvas that agent UIs will eventually need. In the 30-day view, DeepSeek dominates with two entries in the top three. The breakout hit of the month is unslop — a Claude skill that fixes AI-generated UI — proof that the "de-slopping" market is real and growing alongside the slop itself.
