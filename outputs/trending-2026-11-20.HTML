The agent ecosystem is splitting into two layers: runtimes that keep agents alive for days, and skills that plug into those runtimes like apps into a phone. Five of this week's top repos are packaged skill sets — marketing, inference tuning, design, crypto trading — confirming that the "skill as distribution unit" pattern has moved from experiment to default. Meanwhile, the on-device push has jumped from phones to creative workstations: Blender gets an MCP server, Houdini gets a sketch-to-3D bridge, and filmmaking gets a self-hosted studio. The unifying thread is that the model is no longer the product; the integration surface is.
