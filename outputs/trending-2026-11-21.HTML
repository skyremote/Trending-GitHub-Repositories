Six of this week's ten repos ship as installable skills or MCP servers rather than standalone apps, confirming that the distribution unit for AI tooling is now a capability you plug into an existing harness, not a product you switch to. The most telling signal is Blender landing a full MCP addon at nearly 2,000 stars in two days — creative tools are no longer waiting for dedicated AI features; they are absorbing the agent protocol directly. Meanwhile, the 30-day window reveals a parallel arms race in on-device agents: DeepSeek Harness on mobile (29k stars), Mika on Android, and Poki on iPhone all crossed a thousand stars, suggesting users want personal agents that live on the phone, not in a browser tab. If you are building agent infrastructure, the message is clear: target the protocol layer (MCP, skills) and the edge (phone, local GPU), because the browser-hosted chatbot era is compressing fast.
